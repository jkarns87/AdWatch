AdWatch
Competitive intelligence for paid search
DevNetwork API + Cloud + AI Hackathon · API World, September 2026

Know what your competitors did before your standup.

AdWatch watches every Google ad your competitors run, every keyword they bid on, and every shift in category demand — then tells you what changed, why it matters, and what to do next. Built on public data: nothing to connect, no ad-account access.

The problem

Every paid-search team runs the same weekly ritual: open a competitor's transparency page, search your own keywords in an incognito window, check a trends chart, paste screenshots into a deck. It is slow, it is retrospective, and it has no concept of change — you get a snapshot, not a signal. Incumbent intelligence suites cost hundreds of dollars a seat and still deliver a report, not an alert.

The product

WatchCompetitor creatives (Ads Transparency Center), the live paid block on your keywords (Google Search), and demand (Google Trends) — via SerpApi, on a schedule.
DiffEvery run is compared to the last. Nine typed change kinds with severity. The first run is a silent baseline.
ExplainAn AI analyst reads only the structured diff and writes what happened, why it matters, and 2–3 actions with effort and urgency.
Alert & reportHigh-severity insights hit team chat or email. One click renders a CFO spend-&-risk brief or a marketing action brief (PDF, DOCX, MD).

Why now

Ad transparency data became public and, through SerpApi, structured and real-time — the sensor now exists. Language models made "explain the diff" cheap enough to run every cycle. And paid-search budgets are under scrutiny: finance wants competitive context for spend, not just performance dashboards. The wedge is the diff; the moat is the history of it.
